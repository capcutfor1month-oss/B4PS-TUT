# Test User Flow

- Read `docs/FOUNDER_COMMUNICATION.md` and the approved journey.
- Test the approved journey in a local or preview environment.
- Cover important failure states and device sizes.
- Do not use real production data.
- Do not modify code.
- Record steps and evidence in `ux-report.md`.

## Founder-facing return

Before the technical report, explain in plain language:

- Whether the journey felt understandable and worked as intended
- Why the findings matter for the real user experience
- What succeeded and what felt confusing or broken
- What happens next
- What the founder should test, decide, or approve
- The recommended next action and reason

End with:

## What you should do now

## Technical evidence

Include the journey, environment, device size, steps performed, expected result, actual result, evidence, UX problems, functional problems, uncertainty, and permitted next action.
